    Sign in to your router

    Lets the dashboard show how every device is connected: by cable or on 2.4, 5 or 6 GHz Wi-Fi, which access point it uses and how strong its signal is. The login is stored only on this box. It is never shown again or sent anywhere else.

    Open the router's own page ↗ to check the login works there, or to change it.

    Devices to watch on

    Each line is checked every minute and shown on the dashboard. This list belongs to this network only: other networks the box has been on keep their own.

    Paste a list (CSV)

    One device per line: group,name,type,target,description. Types: ping 192.168.1.10, http http://192.168.1.20, port 192.168.1.30:445, dns example.com@192.168.1.1.
